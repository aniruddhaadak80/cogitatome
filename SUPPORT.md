# Support

Questions about cogitatome? Start here.

## Where to ask

- **How do I …?** — [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/aniruddhaadak80/cogitatome/discussions)
  (Q&A category). Search first; the corpus and the guides answer most things.
- **Something is broken** — open a bug report (issue template). Include the
  exact reproduction, your OS, and your `bun --version` / `node --version`.
- **Security problem** — see `SECURITY.md`. Do not open a public issue.
